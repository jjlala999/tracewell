If you’ve lost crypto: what to do now

Act quickly

Exchanges and stablecoin issuers can only freeze funds while they still hold them. Report within hours if you can, not weeks.

Keep your evidence

Save transaction IDs, wallet addresses, chat messages, emails and screenshots. Download the case summary from a trace.

Report it

File a police report and a report with your national fraud or cybercrime service, such as the FBI’s IC3 in the US. Include the case summary.

Beware recovery scams

Anyone who contacts you offering to recover your crypto for an upfront fee is very likely a second scammer. No one can reverse a blockchain transaction.

Read results carefully

A mixer breaks the trail. An exchange is the best lead. An unhosted wallet means no company holds it. Labels are clues, not proof of who a person is.

What this tool can’t see

It follows only a few payments per wallet, follows bridges only when the free LI.FI service recognises them, and can’t name the owners of private wallets.
